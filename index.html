Working with us

What you can bring us, and what we will resolve on your behalf.

Design review, before you tender

Send the elevation at concept or DD stage. We come back with what is buildable as drawn, what will need a joint you have not shown, and roughly what each option costs per square foot.

Material selection and samples

Finish samples in the coating and gauge you are considering, plus a sample panel at real size where the detail warrants it. We will tell you where a finish will not hold up in coastal exposure.

Shop drawings and detailing

We produce fabrication-level shop drawings from your design intent set — joints, tolerances, brackets, interfaces with the RCC frame — and submit them to you for approval before anything is cut.

Structural and performance backup

Wind load and deflection calculations for the system as detailed, and coordination with your structural consultant on fixing points and slab-edge conditions.

Mock-ups

A visual mock-up for finish and sightline approval, and a performance mock-up where the project requires water and air testing. You approve before production begins.

Site coordination

We survey the built frame before fabrication rather than assuming drawn dimensions, and coordinate sequencing with the main contractor so the envelope does not become the critical path.

From enquiry to installation

Four stages. Timing depends on scope, approvals and site readiness, so we work to stage completion rather than fixed weeks.

Stage 1

Drawing review

You send the elevation set and any performance requirements. We review buildability and flag the details that will need resolving.

You receive A buildability note and an indicative rate per square foot.

Stage 2

Detailing and approval

We produce shop drawings and structural backup calculations against your design intent, then revise until you approve.

You receive Shop drawings, load calculations, finish samples, mock-up where required.

Stage 3

Survey and fabrication

We survey the built frame, adjust for actual site dimensions and fabricate. Production starts only after approved drawings and a site survey.

You receive A survey report, a fabrication schedule and inspection access at our works.

Stage 4

Installation and handover

Our own installation crews, sequenced with the main contractor. Snagging closed out before demobilisation.

You receive As-built drawings, test certificates, warranty and a maintenance schedule.

Can you give us CAD details for our drawing set?

Yes. We issue typical sections and junction details in DWG against a specific project once we have seen the elevation. We do not publish a generic library, because a detail that is right for a six-storey building in Chennai is wrong for a coastal tower, and an untailored detail in a tender set causes more problems than it solves.

At what stage should we bring you in?

Concept or early DD is the most useful point. Once the elevation is tendered, the options for resolving a difficult junction narrow to the ones that fit the drawn dimension. Early review costs you nothing and usually changes one or two details.

Do you work as a nominated subcontractor?

Yes, and also directly with the client or under the main contractor. Tell us the contract structure at enquiry stage — it affects how we price and who we coordinate with.

What do you need from us to quote?

Elevations, a typical section, the total facade area, the intended material and finish if decided, and the project location. If those are not resolved yet, send what exists and we will quote a range with the assumptions written down.

Can you match a finish we have specified from another manufacturer?

Usually. Send the reference and we will produce a sample in that coating for approval before committing. Where an exact match is not achievable, we will say so rather than get close and hope.
