Questions architects ask us

At what stage should we bring you in?

Concept or early design development is the most useful point. Once the elevation is tendered, the options for resolving a difficult junction narrow to the ones that fit the drawn dimension. Early review costs you nothing and usually changes one or two details.

Can you give us CAD details for our drawing set?

Yes. We issue typical sections and junction details in DWG against a specific project once we have seen the elevation. We do not publish a generic library, because a detail that is right for a six-storey building inland is wrong for a coastal frontage, and an untailored detail in a tender set causes more problems than it solves.

What do you need from us to quote?

Elevations, a typical section, the total facade area, the intended material and finish if decided, and the project location. If those are not resolved yet, send what exists and we will quote a range with the assumptions written down.

What happens after we send you a drawing set?

We review buildability and come back with an indicative rate per square foot and the details we think will cause trouble, usually within [0] working days. If it proceeds, we produce shop drawings and structural backup calculations for your approval, survey the built structure before fabrication, then install with our own crews and hand over as-builts, test certificates and warranty.

Can you work on an occupied building?

Yes — most of our retrofit work is on buildings that keep trading throughout. We sequence installation around business hours and phase the elevation so the shopfront is never fully hoarded for longer than necessary.

Do you work as a nominated subcontractor?

Yes, and also directly with the client or under the main contractor. Tell us the contract structure at enquiry stage — it affects how we price and who we coordinate with.

Can you match a finish we have specified from another manufacturer?

Usually. Send the reference and we will produce a sample in that coating for approval before committing. Where an exact match is not achievable, we will say so rather than get close and hope.
